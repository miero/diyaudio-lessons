Takeaways and limits

What the demo establishes

  • Restate the answer to the opening question.
  • Tie the visual change to the governing equation.
  • Give one practical consequence.

What it does not establish

  • Name assumptions and synthetic data explicitly.
  • Show at least one failure mode instead of hiding it.
  • Recommend a real measurement when the model is underdetermined.

Check your understanding

Use questions that distinguish the mechanism from a plausible misconception.
